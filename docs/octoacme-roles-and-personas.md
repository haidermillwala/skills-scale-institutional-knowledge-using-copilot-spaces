# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create user-centered experiences and interfaces. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and meet user needs through research and iterative design.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and visual interfaces
- Define interaction patterns and user workflows
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation feasibility
- Establish design systems and guidelines for consistency
- Advocate for user needs and accessibility standards

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce design-to-implementation gaps
- Ensure continuous improvement through user feedback
- Build cohesive, on-brand visual and interaction design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product and Development teams
- User research findings and usability test reports
- Design documentation and component libraries
- Weekly design syncs and feedback sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Product Managers** on feature prioritization and user requirements
- Collaborates with **Developers** on design implementation and technical constraints
- Partners with **QA/Testing** on usability acceptance criteria
- Engages with **Customer Support Lead** for user feedback and pain points

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational reliability. They enable fast, safe releases and ensure systems are scalable, secure, and observable.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage cloud infrastructure and infrastructure-as-code
- Implement monitoring, alerting, and logging solutions
- Automate deployment and operational processes
- Manage secrets, security scanning, and compliance in pipelines
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Collaborate with Developers on deployability and best practices

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments
- Reduce time-to-production and manual operational overhead
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Ensure security and compliance in infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Deployment runbooks and incident playbooks
- Infrastructure documentation and configuration repos
- Pipeline and tooling updates in engineering channels
- On-call escalations and post-incident reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports **Developers** with pipeline setup and operational tooling
- Collaborates with **Project Managers** on release scheduling and deployment windows
- Works with **Security Specialists** on infrastructure security and compliance
- Partners with **QA/Testing** on test automation and staging environments

---

## Security Specialist

### Role Summary
Security Specialists ensure products and processes meet security and compliance requirements. They identify threats, review designs and code, and help teams build security into every stage of development.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and security architecture reviews
- Perform code and design security assessments
- Define security policies and compliance requirements
- Manage security scanning and vulnerability remediation
- Support incident response for security issues
- Provide security training and guidance to teams
- Maintain security documentation and audit trails

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities before production
- Ensure compliance with regulations and standards
- Build a security-aware culture across teams
- Respond quickly to incidents with minimal impact

### Typical Communication
- Security review findings and recommendations
- Threat models and risk assessments
- Security policies and guidelines
- Incident reports and post-mortems

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Reviews code and designs with **Developers** and **Product Managers**
- Collaborates with **DevOps Engineers** on infrastructure security and secrets management
- Works with **QA/Testing** on security test automation and penetration testing
- Partners with **Project Managers** on security-related risk mitigation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They gather requirements, map processes, and help teams understand the business context and expected outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements
- Map current and future business processes
- Define acceptance criteria alongside Product Managers
- Analyze business data and impact
- Create requirement specifications and process documentation
- Support stakeholder communication and alignment
- Identify process improvements and optimization opportunities

### Goals
- Ensure solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce misalignment between business and technical teams
- Enable informed decision-making with clear requirement documentation
- Drive process improvements and operational efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Process flow diagrams and business case analysis
- Stakeholder meeting notes and alignment summaries
- Impact assessments and business metrics reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Product Managers** on requirement definition and prioritization
- Collaborates with **Project Managers** on scope and timeline planning
- Supports **Developers** with detailed requirement clarification
- Engages with **Customer Support Lead** for customer context and feedback

---

## Customer Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support Leads represent the voice of the customer and user. They channel feedback into the project team, identify support gaps, and ensure solutions meet customer needs and expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze customer feedback and support tickets
- Identify trends, pain points, and feature requests
- Communicate customer needs to Product and Development teams
- Define support runbooks and documentation
- Plan customer communication for releases and changes
- Support customer escalations and issue resolution
- Measure customer satisfaction and support metrics

### Goals
- Minimize customer escalations and support burden
- Ensure customer needs inform product decisions
- Deliver excellent customer experience and satisfaction
- Build strong customer relationships and trust

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Support runbooks and documentation
- Release announcements and customer communications
- Customer satisfaction metrics and insights

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Provides feedback to **Product Managers** on customer needs and market signals
- Collaborates with **Developers** and **QA/Testing** on support documentation and edge cases
- Works with **Project Managers** on customer communication during releases
- Partners with **Business Analysts** on customer pain point analysis

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts measure, analyze, and communicate the impact of product decisions. They build dashboards, identify trends, and provide data-driven insights to inform strategy and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Design and build analytics dashboards and reports
- Track success metrics and KPIs
- Analyze user behavior and product usage patterns
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities
- Create data-driven recommendations for Product teams
- Support A/B testing and experimentation
- Ensure data quality and integrity

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making across teams
- Measure and demonstrate product impact and ROI
- Identify optimization and growth opportunities
- Support evidence-based prioritization and strategy

### Typical Communication
- Analytics dashboards and reports
- Trend analyses and insights
- Experiment results and recommendations
- Data documentation and data dictionary

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** on success metrics and data-driven prioritization
- Supports **Project Managers** with velocity and delivery metrics
- Collaborates with **Developers** on instrumentation and data quality
- Works with **Customer Support Lead** on customer satisfaction metrics and trends

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference the interaction patterns to understand cross-functional dependencies and communication flows.
- When running projects, map team members to these personas to clarify roles, responsibilities, and expectations.
